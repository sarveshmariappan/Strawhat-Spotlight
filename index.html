01

MONKEY D. LUFFY

Captain

The founder and captain of the Straw Hat Pirates. Driven by a burning desire for absolute freedom, he set sail on a quest to locate the legendary One Piece and claim the title of King of the Pirates. Powered by the Hito Hito no Mi, Model: Nika, he fights with elastic creativity and an indomitable spirit.

02

RORONOA ZORO

Swordsman

A former bounty hunter who was the first member to join Luffy's crew. Master of the formidable Santoryu (Three Sword Style), Zoro fights using a sword in each hand and one clamped in his mouth. He is bound by an ironclad oath to become the World's Strongest Swordsman, honoring a childhood promise.

฿ 1,111,000,000
03

NAMI

Navigator

A brilliant cartographer and the navigator of the crew. Nami possesses an almost supernatural ability to sense shifts in the weather and navigate through the chaotic, unpredictable seas of the Grand Line. Her lifelong dream is to draw a complete map of the entire world.

BOUNTY
฿ 366,000,000
04

USOPP

Sniper

The crew's sharp-shooting sniper and a naturally gifted storyteller. Though plagued by cowardice in the face of mortal peril, Usopp fights to overcome his fears, striving to fulfill his dream of becoming a proud, courageous warrior of the sea. Armed with custom Pop Green seeds, his accuracy is unmatched.

฿ 500,000,000
05

SANJI

Cook

The gourmet chef of the Straw Hat crew and a master of high-velocity martial arts. Sanji fights exclusively using his legs to safeguard his hands, which he reserves solely for cooking culinary masterpieces. Driven by passion, he searches for the legendary All Blue—a sea containing every fish species in the world.

BOUNTY
฿ 1,032,000,000
06

TONY TONY CHOPPER

Doctor

A blue-nosed reindeer who ate the Hito Hito no Mi (Human-Human Fruit), granting him human intelligence and shape-shifting abilities. Trained by his mentor Dr. Hiluluk, Chopper is a genius physician. He is determined to cure any disease and travel the world to expand his medical knowledge.

07

NICO ROBIN

Archaeologist

The brilliant archaeologist of the crew and the only living person capable of reading the ancient Poneglyphs. Armed with the power of the Hana Hana no Mi (Flower-Flower Fruit), she can sprout multiple limbs on any surface. Robin seeks to uncover the Rio Poneglyph and reveal the true, hidden history of the Void Century.

BOUNTY
฿ 930,000,000
08

FRANKY

Shipwright

The shipwright of the Straw Hats, Franky is a cola-powered cyborg who designed and built the crew's dream ship, the Thousand Sunny. With a larger-than-life personality and heavy-duty arsenal, his dream is to sail his masterpiece to the absolute end of the Grand Line, maintaining it along every mile.

BOUNTY
฿ 394,000,000
09

BROOK

Musician

A gentleman skeletal musician and master fencer who ate the Yomi Yomi no Mi (Revive-Revive Fruit), allowing him to return to life as a skeleton. Brandishing soul-chilling ice attacks and beautiful, haunting melodies, Brook seeks to sail the Grand Line and return to Reverse Mountain to reunite with Laboon the island whale.

BOUNTY
฿ 383,000,000
10

JINBE

Helmsman

A powerful whale shark fish-man and master of Fish-Man Karate, Jinbe is the crew's wise, veteran helmsman. Formerly a Warlord of the Sea, he pledged his life to Luffy, sharing the dream of bringing about true peace and equality between humans and fish-men across the oceans.
